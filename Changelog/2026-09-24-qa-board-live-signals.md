# 2026-09-24 — Q&A Board: live room signals, top voted, pop-up latest

- **What:** the display now shows labeled live stats at top right: reactions (each new one floats up the screen), people online, and question count. It also has a "Someone is typing" indicator. A **Top voted** footer slot shows the highest-voted *unanswered* question that isn't already on screen. The latest question has three modes: **pop-up** (default; a "New question" banner shows for 8s, one at a time, replaying at most the newest 3 after a burst), **always** (a fixed footer slot), and **off**. The latest question and the join link are larger (2.4vw and 2.6vw).
- **Sources (all GET/read-only):** people online and typing come from AhaSlides' own presenter widgets on the same page (`.user-online-status`, and `.chat-bubble.show`, which only appears in present mode). Reaction totals come from `/api/aha-sync/kv/paths?path=/ST2/livestats-default-prd-us01/REACTION_COUNT/PER_REACTION_TYPE/<deck>/1` (the counters are per slide, and the Q&A slide is slide 1).
- **Fixes:** the panel no longer redraws while a mouse button is down, since a redraw mid-click swallowed the "Open display window" click during testing. The build `\u`-escapes all non-ASCII characters, so emoji survive any decoding path. The "online" label no longer repeats.
- **Tested:** the whole build ran in a local mock with made-up questions: layout, labels, emoji, typing, top voted, and the pop-up slide-in and slide-out. The live stats were checked on the real deck earlier in the session. The floating reactions weren't caught in a screenshot.
