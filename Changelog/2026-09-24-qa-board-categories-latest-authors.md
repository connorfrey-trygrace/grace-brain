# 2026-09-24 — Q&A Board: finer categories, permanent latest slot, authors, card sizing

- **Categories split (10 → 16):** Hearing God & the Spirit became **Hearing God**, **The Holy Spirit** and **Discernment & decisions**. Suffering became **Suffering & pain** and **Does God care about me?**. Sovereignty became **God's control & free will** and **Purpose & calling**. Church became **Life at Grace** and **Words, names & basics**. **Death & the end times** was split out of Everything else. "Is Jesus the only way?" became **Salvation & the only way** and picked up the "doomed / lose your salvation" rows. The 84 seeded questions in `Workflows/qa-board/theme-seed.json` were re-sorted by hand, and the keyword rules were rewritten to match. Saved overrides pointing at a retired key (`D`) are ignored.
- **Latest question has a permanent home:** the footer shows Top voted | Latest | join link, and a one-time `latestV` flag switches saved boards to `always`. When a new question arrives, its slot flashes Active Blue for 3.2s with a slight scale bump, the text slides in, and the label reads **Just in** for 20s before settling back to "Latest question". The pop-up banner is still available from the panel's Latest button.
- **Authors:** the Top voted and Latest slots show the submitter's name, or "Anonymous", after the vote count.
- **Card sizing:** the grid now uses `align-items:start`, so each card sizes to its own text instead of stretching to the tallest card in its row.
- **Footer:** the slot text is 2 lines at 1.7vw (room freed by the two-card layout), the join link is 2.4vw, and "Someone is typing" was shortened to "Typing" and moved onto the Latest slot.
- **Tested:** in the local mock: the arrival flash was caught mid-animation, the cards were individually sized, and the new categories showed in the sidebar.
- **Follow-up:** every audience question is now attributed on the display: the QOTN, the rotating cards, curated cards, Top voted, Latest, and the pop-up. It shows the submitter name, or **Anon** in place of "Anonymous" (the panel says Anon too). Host cards carry no attribution. All attributions use one small style: Lato Black, 0.75vw, tracked 0.16em, Cinder Gray (white on teal). The typing indicator moved to sit above "Ask yours at", because it was being clipped next to the Latest author.
