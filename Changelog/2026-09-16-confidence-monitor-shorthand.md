# The confidence monitor has a fixed size, and the 9/20 host notes were written as if it didn't

`Confidence Monitor Template.docx` (Connor, 2026-09-16) is a Word file with a fixed-size text box that shows exactly how much text fits on the confidence monitor: **20pt Calibri, roughly 8 lines × 40 characters (~300 chars)**. Its sample text is written in telegraphic shorthand — *"Tough yr. When life hard, who call?"* — which is the real instruction: CM text is cue text, not prose. The stated process is type into the box → Save As → email to **Brian Parker** to load onto the monitor → print it to rehearse against.

Filed to `/Reference` (it is the standing container for every service, not a 9/20 artifact). It arrived at the vault root the same day the root cleanup ran.

**The gap it exposed:** all four Host items on the 9/20 PCO plan were labelled "Confidence Monitor Notes" and written in full sentences. Measured against the box, `Host Transition → Host` (seq 10) came to **10 wrapped lines — over by 2**, and the overflow was the AhaSlides instruction, which ate four lines on its own. The other three fit but read as paragraphs, not cues.

All four rewritten in CM shorthand in [plan 91130954](https://services.planningcenteronline.com/plans/91130954) — seq 2 Host Welcome, seq 10 Host, seq 14 Host (Q&A), seq 16 Host (Peer Discussion). Every item is now ≤8 lines with no line over 40 characters, so nothing wraps unpredictably on the monitor. Two things were deliberately **not** abbreviated: the QOTN itself (it is read aloud verbatim, not cued) and `FEEDBACK FORM` (already a shout-cue).

**Found in passing, and it is a real dependency:** seq 14 carries an Audio/Visual note nobody had surfaced — `SLIDE (1920x1080 PNG): Dropbox > Sunday Visuals > Sunday Night > "9.20.26 - Question of the Night.png"`. The QOTN was only decided tonight, so that PNG either does not exist yet or does not carry the final wording. It is not tracked as an action anywhere.
