# 2026-09-24 — Q&A Board: "Same category" cycling under the QOTN

- **What:** a new board mode, **Same category**, is now the default; a one-time `relatedV` flag switches saved boards over to it. The cards under the question of the night are every other question in the QOTN's category, answered or not, minus hidden ones, most-voted first. The board shows as many whole cards as fit and advances to the next set every 10s, starting right after the last card that fit. The label reads "You've been asking · <category>". Curated and Live top 6 remain as panel options. A host-card QOTN has no category, so the panel says to pick an audience question.
- **Space:** the footer's Top voted slot is now one line (2.1vw), and card padding and gaps are tighter, so a two-line QOTN leaves room for 2 rows (4 cards) at 16:9.
- **Fixes found in testing:**
  - The fit check measured cards mid-entrance-animation (via `getBoundingClientRect`, which includes the translateY) and hid them for good. It now measures layout boxes, re-runs once web fonts load, and re-runs on every 2s stats tick.
  - A `focusout` handler reset the pointer-down guard mid-click, so a background redraw could still swallow a button press.
- **Tested:** in the local mock with the QOTN in "Suffering & whether God cares" (15 related questions). Page 1 showed votes 15/12/9/6 and page 2 showed 5/4/4/3, as whole cards with a staggered entrance.
- **Revision (same day, Connor's feedback):** the rotation is now gradual. The screen holds **two cards**, and every 7s one of them (alternating left/right) fades out over 0.9s and the next question in the category fades in, walking the category by votes and wrapping at the end. This replaces the whole-page swap every 10s. The two slots are updated in place, and vote counts refresh without a fade. The footer's Top voted and Latest picks no longer exclude the rotating cards, so the footer doesn't redraw on each swap. The **Top categories** list only touches the DOM when the ranking changes, and it re-inserts rows only when their order changes; re-inserting had been replaying the rows' fade-in on every rotation. The subtitle is now "By number of questions", with no "Unanswered"; the sidebar still counts only unanswered questions. Tested in the local mock: L 15→9, R 12→6, L 9→5, with the sidebar untouched.
- **Revision 2:** the rotation animation is now a quiet crossfade: opacity only, 1.1s out and 1.4s in, with no slide and no teal border flash. Teal plus motion is reserved for real new submissions (the Latest slot's "Just in" flash), so the rotating cards no longer read as fresh arrivals. The first two cards also fade in, staggered 300ms.
- **Revision 3 (the "cards synced up" report):** the cause was a long question rotating into a slot where it didn't fit above the footer. The fit check hid it until that slot's next turn 14s later, which read as one card missing and then both changing at once. Fix: questions over 140 characters render at 1.2vw, and a rotating card that still doesn't fit is replaced on the spot by the next question in the category that does. The replacement happens on swap, on first fill, and on every fit check (font load, resize, the 2s stats tick). Questions too long to fit at all are skipped, not shown hidden. Verified in the mock over 36s: strict left/right alternation every 7s, no hidden card, and an ~700-character question skipped.
- **Revision 4:** each rotating card is now a stable box with stacked content layers. On a swap, the old text fades out over 560ms, then the new text fades in over 840ms, while the card's outline stays at full opacity. The whole card no longer fades out, which had left one slot empty for about 2.5s per swap and made one card look missing. A true simultaneous crossfade was tried and dropped because the two questions overlapped into an unreadable jumble. The fit check doesn't hide a card mid-swap. Measured in the mock: 48 mid-swap samples with 0 moments of overlapping text, and the card box never dropped below full opacity.
- **Also:** a **Votes** stat (the total up-votes across every approved question, answered included) sits in the top right after Questions.
