# The Q&A tool decision took six weeks to arrive where the research started

## What happened

Connor, 2026-08-18: *"We're actually using aha slides. Way cheaper."* — reversing, within a few hours, the Slido choice he had reaffirmed the same day.

## The path this decision actually took

| Date | State |
|---|---|
| 7/06 | Research recommends **AhaSlides Pro** (~$16/mo, unlimited) over Slido Enterprise (~$150/mo). Capacity requirement raised to **500+** the same day. |
| 7/08 | **Decided: Slido.** Changelog records *"Reason not stated."* |
| 7/08 | Reopened — the ~10x cost gap registers; Connor to re-look at AhaSlides. **Never closed.** |
| 8/12 | The John Slye call decides a **Slido tier** in passing — *"run the sub-300 tier now"* — while the AhaSlides re-look is still open. Neither note acknowledges the other. |
| 8/17 | The contradiction is found while closing the run-of-show item, and the gate turns out to be **untracked entirely** — it lived as an unchecked box on a 17 KB event note. |
| 8/18 | Connor: Slido. Then, hours later: **AhaSlides.** |

## Why it mattered that this drifted

Two things were wrong simultaneously and neither was visible from inside either note:

1. **A load-bearing paid deadline had no tracker item.** *Sept 7 — tool picked, paid, tested on the auditorium screen* sat in a table on an event note, so no Open Actions sweep ever saw it. It surfaced only because a different item was being closed.
2. **The tier reasoning contradicted the capacity requirement.** *"Sub-300 tier"* (8/12) against *"capacity 500+"* (7/06). Slido Professional caps at 300, and a participant cap is a **hard fail, not a degrade** — the 301st person opens the link and cannot submit, silently to everyone except them.

## What the reversal buys

≈**$1,600/yr**, and the removal of three separate problems rather than one: the 300-participant cap, the "decide the upgrade by 9/14" constraint, and the unreconciled sub-300-vs-500+ tension. AhaSlides Pro has one plan and no ladder.

Pricing re-verified 2026-08-18 against ahaslides.com and Capterra — unchanged from the 7/06 research: Free 50, Essential $7.95/mo (100), **Pro $15.95/mo ("Unlimited audience size")**, Enterprise custom.

## The caveat that changed weight

The 7/06 research flagged *"unlimited"* as a marketing claim needing written confirmation. That flag was cheap when Slido was the fallback. **It is not cheap now** — unlimited capacity is the entire reason this is safe against John's 225+ plausible base, so an undisclosed fair-use ceiling would swap a known 300 cap for an unknown one. AhaSlides' own "Conferences" use case cites up to 2,500, so the risk reads low, but low is not confirmed. Ask hi@ahaslides.com before 9/07, along with the nonprofit rate — their docs name *"non-profit organizations"* without explicitly naming religious ones.
