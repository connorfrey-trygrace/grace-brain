## What she brought to the Sunday-night problem *(2026-09-20)*

- **The diagnosis:** *"One of the things people love, it helps them feel secure, is knowing what's coming. Knowing what's next, the expectations… What this really is all about is making people feel secure and that this is a safe space. When they don't know, they feel uneasy."* Her own practice is to publish the agenda in advance — for a women's conference, down to *"I'm allowed to take a bathroom break at 11 o'clock, which sounds so stupid, but I'm serious that we put it right in there."* This became the debrief's most-agreed idea: [[Archive/Actions/Connor - Signpost the 9-27 run-of-show to the room]]
- **An alternative format nobody has chosen between.** Grace's own older pattern, which she surfaced: John preaches, one worship song, **the service formally closes**, and *"if you'd like to stay, we're gonna have a Q&A"* — everyone remaining moves to the front and John sits on the edge of the stage to lead it. Her argument is that the formal close removes the pressure: *"if you want to go home, you can go home without the pressure of — if it's time to pick up kids or whatever."* This is the **opposite bet** from the 9/27 plan, which front-loads the Q&A to hold people through the message. The 9/27 shape won by default, not by decision — see [[Events/2026-09-20-Sunday-Night-Service/2026-09-20-Sunday-Night-Service-Launch]].
