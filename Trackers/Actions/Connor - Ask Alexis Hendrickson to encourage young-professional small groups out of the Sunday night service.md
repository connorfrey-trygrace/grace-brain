- **Whoever actually rose.** John Slye's 8/07 email floated naming two leaders up front; Connor's call (2026-08-12) is that nobody gets named in advance — leaders will surface on their own across the eight weeks and **Connor keeps the pulse on that**. So the December ask is built from real names with real evidence, not a slate picked in August. **If a group has already started informally by then, this ask changes shape from "please start this" to "please absorb and support what already started."**
