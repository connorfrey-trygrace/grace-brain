## 🎤 Halftime Trivia Question Bank (30 — expanded 2026-07-18)

Ordered easy → hard; mark ✋ = gimme. Verify the two ⚠️ 2026-specific answers before event day. **Questions 13–30 added 7/18** as backup gimmes so the game keeps moving if the harder original questions (9–12) stump the room — skew easier, pull from these first if energy dips. 2. ✋ What's the trophy called? *(The FIFA World Cup Trophy — and it's solid gold)*
3. ✋ How often is the World Cup held? *(Every 4 years)*
4. ⚠️ What stadium is hosting today's final? *(MetLife Stadium, New Jersey — confirm)*
5. Which country has won the most World Cups coming into this tournament? *(Brazil — 5)*
6. This is the first World Cup with how many teams? *(48 — up from 32)*
7. What year did the U.S. last host the men's World Cup? *(1994)*
8. Which two countries have played in the most finals? *(Germany — 8, Brazil — 7)*
9. Who has scored the most goals in World Cup history (entering 2026)? *(Miroslav Klose — 16)*
10. What's the fastest goal in World Cup history? *(Hakan Şükür, ~11 seconds, 2002)*
11. Only one country has won back-to-back World Cups since WWII. Which? *(Brazil — 1958, 1962)*
12. ⚠️ Tiebreaker (closest number wins): total goals scored in this World Cup before today's final? *(**297 goals in 102 matches as of 7/15** [FIFA stats] — but this is NOT final: the France–England 3rd-place match plays tonight, 7/18, 5 PM ET, and its goals count toward the total.
