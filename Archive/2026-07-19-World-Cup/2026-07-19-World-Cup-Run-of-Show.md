## 👔 Second-Half Hydration Break — Best Dressed (~3 min)

FIFA mandates one 3-minute hydration break per half tournament-wide this year (heat rule) — the ref calls it at the next stoppage after roughly the half's midpoint. For a 3 PM kickoff, expect it somewhere **~4:35–4:45 PM**, moving with the flow of play. **No crowd cheer, no runner-up trophies** — you've already picked the winner. Announce, reveal the gummy bear (keep it hidden until now — the unveil is the bit), hand it over. Fast: nothing else to run in the 3-minute window.

## 🎤 Halftime Script (~15 min)

1. **Trivia** (5–7 min) — pull from the 30-question bank (host card: [[2026-07-19-World-Cup-Trivia-Host-Card]]). One buzzer per table; first buzz answers, wrong answer freezes that table out for the question. Start with the original easy → hard 12; drop into the 18 backup gimmes (Q13–30) if energy dips or the room stalls on a hard one. Winning table gets a $35 gift card (split/RPS — say so up front).
2. **Prediction teaser** — remind the room the closest score wins at full time (first-goal minute breaks ties); form's still open.

## 🏁 Final Whistle

- **Prediction card winner** — pull the Google Sheet, sort by closest combined score (tiebreaker: closest first-goal minute) → $35 gift card.
- **Slalom challenge** — no single winner to announce; trophies already handed out live all event to anyone who finished the cone course.
- **Host's choice wildcard** — award the 3rd $35 card live, for whatever earned it (loudest table, best comeback, etc.) — your call, any time during or after the game.
