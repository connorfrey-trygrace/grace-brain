- **Notes**: Design settled in conversation, superseding the earlier "designate a fixed subset of tables as open for the whole session" idea (rejected — Connor: "this is not a session by session system for newcomers, this has to be week by week"). Landed model: each week, one table (and its leader) is the designated **accepting table** — that leader becomes "greeting team leader" for the week, and the whole table stands outside the entrance to welcome arrivals and invite anyone who doesn't already have a table to sit with them. Detection and placement happen in one motion — no separate door-greeter team, no live cross-table headcount tracking. Once placed, a newcomer stays with that table going forward.
  Three things still need an answer once sign-ups are in:
  1. **Rotation order** — who's in the pool of accepting-table leaders, and in what order across the 8 weeks (spread the load so no one table absorbs newcomers every week).
  2. **Backup plan** — what happens if the assigned leader can't make it a given Wednesday (standing second, or skip the week).
  3. **Reconcile with the sign-up text's framing** — the text Connor already sent asked people to pick a "Greeting team" as if it were a standing crew; this model reframes that into "willing to take rotation weeks as the accepting table." Need to decide whether to clarify with the group or just treat Greeting sign-ups as opting into the rotation.
