# 📈 Kay Carter-Corker: Capture Connect Opportunity attendance in Rock

- [ ] Work out **where the Connect Opportunity (ConnOpp) head count is captured, stored and displayed** in Rock — deliberately **off** the home-page adult-attendance total.

- **Source**: [[Transcripts/2026-08-28 10.35 Rock–PCO Tag Teams Resolved — Sunday Night Attendance Metrics]] — Connor asked in the room; Kay took it: *"let me think through how to do that and where you capture that number, where the number is stored, and where it's visible"* and *"we can figure out how that will happen for you."*
- **What Connor wants and why**: a second Sunday-night number — how many people **stay** after the message for the ConnOpp (peer discussion + question of the night). It is his success measure for the connection half of John Slye's *content and connection* design brief. He expects it **every Sunday going forward**, not just during the break-in weeks: *"It might look different, but there will be one every Sunday."*
- **Connor's proposed shape**: a **5th service type** — `Sunday Night 6 PM` and `Sunday Night ConnOpp` — riding the same usher QR form as the other services.
- **⚠️ The one hard constraint, agreed by both**: it must **not** roll into the home-page adult-attendance total. The morning figures can be summed safely because nobody attends two services; every ConnOpp attendee was **already counted at 6 PM**, so adding it double-counts them. Kay's first instinct was to put it on the home page and Connor talked her off it; she accepted. Where the number *does* surface is the open part of the question.
- **Priority rationale**: P2, not the P1 the Connor-assigned floor would give. Connor explicitly softened the ask himself — *"if this is a square peg for a round hole, that's okay"* — which is the stated override the floor allows. It is a measurement instrument, not a launch dependency: the service runs without it.
- **Deadline rationale**: 9/20 is the first Sunday there is a ConnOpp to count. Not invented — Connor tied it to the launch, not to a date he picked.
