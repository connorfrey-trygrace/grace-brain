## Open w/ Psalm 23 (1237916291)
“The Lord is my shepherd, I lack nothing.
He makes me lie down in green pastures, He leads me beside still waters,
He refreshes my soul.”

What stands out to me in this passage is that it says "He makes me" lie down. He doesn’t suggest I lay down, He doesn’t offer it as an option. He makes me. 
The psalmist is showing that stillness is the first step in refreshing our souls - mind, will, and emotions.

If you're anything like me, you're already thinking about tomorrow. I am a do-er, and I really struggle to turn that off. Sometimes I need to be MADE to sit. 

Tomorrow is gonna be fast and busy, but this is a moment where we can be still and get refreshed, so that we have what we need for tomorrow. So we're going to pray and then just take a minute to intentionally sit in that stillness.

## Reflection (1237916296)
If God is who He says He is, what does that mean for us? 
He says he’s enough, his grace is sufficient, and that his power is made perfect in our weakness. 
Where we fall short, he completes us and makes up the difference. If He truly is enough, it’s impossible for us to not be enough. 
If I bring 90, he brings 10. If it's a day where I can only manage to bring 10, He brings 90. 
Those are His words. That’s what He said. If God is enough, I’m enough. This next song is called 'Home". It's a Grace original, and I encourage you to make these words your own.

## Invitation Into Exchange (1237916298)
We are not mere servants, we are sons and daughters, which means we have security, privileges, rights, and inheritance. That is all possible because of the finished work of Jesus on the cross. 
That’s the greatest act of love in history. If He never did one more thing for us, it would be enough. But he doesn’t stop with enough. He came so the we could have life to the fullest, and he knows we can’t do that with all the things we’re carrying. 
1 Peter 5:7 says to "cast your burdens on him because he cares for you", so we are going to create the space so that each of us can do that intentionally.
There are postcards under your seats, if you’re comfortable, I'd like you to write down those burdens that are pressing most on you. It could be fear, doubt, anxiety, insecurity, grief. 
Once you write them all down, tear it up. We're going to pass plates down each row; drop it in the plate as an offering to God. When you're finished, I invite you to stand and sing when you're ready.
