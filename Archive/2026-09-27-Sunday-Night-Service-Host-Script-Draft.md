## 1. OPENING — the signposting block

Goes in the **Host Welcome** item, plan 91453887 seq 2, which currently reads only `Welcome!\nEvening service\nTheme: How Is Jesus Here?`.

This is the fix for the thing the whole debrief converged on: 9/20 ran 1h40m against a 50-55 minute norm and about 50 of 149 people left in a trickle from 6:55 on. Krista Slye's point was that people feel secure when they know what is coming; Connor's was the YouTube chapter bar. The room never got told what it had walked into.

**Three rules for delivering it, which matter more than the wording:**

1. **Say the end time out loud.** "We'll wrap up around 7:20." That single sentence is the whole intervention. A range people can plan around beats an accurate agenda they cannot.
2. **Name the off-ramp in the same breath.** The Lobby discussion is optional and has to sound optional when it is announced at the *start*, not apologised for at the end.
3. **Don't read the table.** Four beats, conversational. A recited agenda is worse than none.

### Confidence monitor block (paste into seq 2)

```
Confidence Monitor Notes:
Welcome - week 2 of Sunday night
Intro names
Here's the shape of tonight:
-> ~20 min worship
-> question of the night + popcorn
-> message - "Where Do You Turn?"
-> communion
Done around 7:20
After: optional Lobby hangout w/ discussion
Theme: How Is Jesus Here?
```

### If a host wants it as prose

> "Hey, welcome back - week two. Quick map of the night so you know what you're in for: about twenty minutes of worship, then we're doing the question of the night and handing out popcorn, then John's message, then communion. We'll be done around 7:20. And after that, if you want to stick around, we'll be out in the Lobby with some discussion questions up on the screens - totally optional, no pressure."
