## Why it reads the way it does

Decisions made in drafting, recorded so a later edit doesn't silently undo them:

- **The gathering is optional; the contact is not.** "Get the invite out to everyone at your table, not just the guys you already talk to" is the load-bearing line — it makes whole-table contact the real requirement while leaving the gathering itself soft. A leader can't satisfy this by texting his two friends. It also picks up the anti-cliques concern raised at the 7/19 lunch without naming anyone.
- **No scripture anchor, deliberately.** Earlier drafts closed on a Jesus comparison. Two problems, both Connor's catch. (1) The first version — "Jesus had the twelve with him long before he taught them anything" — is **factually wrong**: Jesus was preaching before he called Simon and Andrew (Mark 1:14–20) and taught in the Capernaum synagogue immediately after (Mark 1:21–22); the appointing of the twelve (Mark 3:13–19) follows extensive teaching. Presence and teaching ran together from the start. (2) Even corrected, the framing casts the leader as the itinerant rabbi and his table as *his* disciples — borrowing Christ's **office** rather than his **posture**. Imitation of Christ is commanded (John 13:15, 1 Cor 11:1, 1 Pet 2:21), but Scripture runs it toward the downward posture — servanthood, going to people — and when Paul models it he does so as a fellow servant ("follow my example, as I follow the example of Christ"). Alternatives considered and held in reserve: Prov 27:17 (iron sharpens iron — horizontal, already in the boot camp theme material) and 1 Thess 2:8 (Paul sharing "not only the gospel but our lives as well"). Cut on the judgment that the ask needs no warrant for this audience.
- **Opens by absorbing the low reply rate.** "A lot of homework" places it on the weight of the 7/24 ask (two questions plus a five-option list) rather than on the leaders being slow. Neutral alternative if ever needed: "Quick one, unrelated to my last text."
- **No dependency created for Connor.** An earlier draft offered to look up who was at each table; cut, since the rosters aren't in the vault and it would have bounced the work back to him. Also cut: "the fall is handled on my end / this stretch is the part only you can cover" — it made Connor the hinge and read heavier than the moment warranted.
- **The seven-week gap is not mentioned.** Naming it invites "so why isn't anything planned?" — a fair question given the [[Events/2026-07-29-Boot-Camp/2026-07-29-Boot-Camp-Summer-Camp]] cancellation. "No reason to wait for September" carries the timing without opening that door.
- Per standing practice: no wikilinks and no per-idea attribution to named leaders in outbound comms.
