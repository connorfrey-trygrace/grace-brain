## 🎯 Program (decided at the 7/19 leaders' lunch)

- **Structure:** keep starting as one large group on stage, then break into small tables — endorsed as still working.
- **Small groups:** keep the **same table** week-to-week (continuity/intimacy outweighed variety in feedback); guys are free to switch to a new table for this new 8-week session if they want, just not required to.
- **New guys mid-session:** designate a subset of tables as "receiving" tables willing to take a new person, so the rest can stay a stable, known group. **Connor is designing the system** to identify and route first-timers to a receiving table before they even sit down (tracked below).
- **Facilitator per table:** explicitly called out as necessary — Kirk Salvary described a table of 7 guys that sat in total silence without one.
- **Discussion questions:** cut down to **1-3 deep, personal questions** instead of a printed list of 5-8 — multiple leaders (Taylor Pearson, Jeff Brady) said guys "speed run" through long lists and it feels rushed.
- **Stage content:** keep the personal-testimony "story" format (well received — "story in the shed" idea: guys not ready for the big stage can still share theirs at the table); keep any story time concise/topic-tied rather than open-ended.
- **Optional post-session mingle:** 15-30 min at the end for guys who want to meet people outside their own table — not mandatory, framed as "stick around if you want."
- **Book:** Gospel of John, verse-by-verse, one section a week — chosen to sync with the "Why It's All About Jesus" sermon series.
- **Avoid cliques** — explicit caution raised (Jeff Brady) about the room feeling closed-off to newcomers.

### Cost-shifting to participants — Derek's position (2026-08-04)

Source: [[Transcripts/2026-08-04 15.59 Connor–Derek Weekly Sync — Expense Codes Locked & Boot Camp Buy-In]]

Connor raised the thing the 7/19 lunch never got to: *"how to shift some of that — the burden of that cost to the participants."* John Slye's prior stance (pre-lunch) was that Grace **should not** fully subsidize Boot Camp concessions. What Derek added:

- **Coding:** Boot Camp food goes under **8105 — men's events** (Community Life). Derek's reasoning for keeping it there rather than under groups: *"We don't really have a budget for, like, just groups to have food… church would be broke with how much it costs to eat out in DC."*
- **Grace will absorb a loss on principle.** *"We are certainly happy to run a loss… we would see it as a financial investment in the future of men, especially young men. If you can get people to show up and food brings them, we would say it's worth it at some level. It's the cost of discipleship."*
- **But Grace is also "wide open to charging" — for the right reason.** Derek's hard bar, verbatim: *"if at the end of the day, the only problem that that really solves for us is not having so much of a loss on the food for an 8-week session, I'm not interested. I'm interested in what will move men to be better followers of Jesus."*
