# 🥾 Boot Camp Vision Lunch Recap
**Sunday, July 19, 2026 · Grace Kids space · ~1 hour · no agenda, open floor**

A quick-reference recap of the leaders' vision-setting lunch, for anyone who missed it or wants the highlights without reading the full transcript.

---

## Who was there

Connor Frey and John Slye, plus 11 Boot Camp leaders: Kirk "Patrick" Salvary, Nico Gomez, Nathan Ebikwo, Taylor Pearson, Donovan Bonner, Brian Sulc, Brandon Geddes, Jeff Brady, Mike Riggs, Simba Kanjanda, and Xavier Swank.

## Why we met

No formal agenda — an open review of what worked (and didn't) across sessions 1 (Judges) and 2 (Ephesians), to shape session 3: the **fall relaunch, Wednesday, September 16**, an 8-week run syncing with the new "Why It's All About Jesus" sermon series (launches Sunday, September 13).

## What we're keeping

- **Big group → small tables.** Still the right shape — stage time first, then break into tables.
- **Same table, same guys, week to week.** Consistency beat novelty in the feedback — guys open up more with people they already know. (Free to pick a new table for this new session if you want, just not required to.)
- **Personal-testimony "stage stories."** Well loved — keep them, but keep them concise and tied to a theme rather than open-ended. *John's notes (7/22) add: if we're leaning further into stories over teaching, we need simple guidelines so speakers stay focused and engaging instead of rambling.*
- **The Wednesday, 7 PM slot.** No appetite to change it.
- **A facilitator at every table.** Not a new change — this is existing practice, per Connor. Kirk's story about a table of 7 guys sitting in dead silence is a reminder of why it matters, not a call for something new.

## What we're changing

- **One question per table, not a printed list.** ✅ **Decided (Connor, 2026-07-24):** defaulting to John's notes (7/22) — **one** primary question a night, with the story, Scripture, and everything else building toward it. This supersedes the room's live discussion, which had loosely converged on 1-3 (Taylor Pearson's suggestion, echoed by Simba Kanjanda's "one simple question") — guys were "speed-running" through long lists instead of actually talking, and John's tighter read is what we're going with.
- **Real thought into icebreakers.** New from John's notes (7/22) — better icebreaker, better discussion, especially in the early weeks. Touched on live too (Nathan Ebikwo raised it, John agreed it's "exactly what we need to do") but didn't make the original cut here.
- **The book: Gospel of John**, studied verse-by-verse, one section a week, timed to track the new sermon series.
- **Watch the cliques.** Explicit callout (Jeff Brady) — new guys can feel like outsiders fast if tables read as already-closed friend groups.

## Ideas on the table (not decided yet)

- **Leader + co-leader per table** — John floated this live (not committed); his notes (7/22) add specificity — pair an older guy with a younger guy, and have them stay in touch with their table through the week (text, a verse, a prayer, just checking in), not just at the Wednesday meetup. This folds in what was "outside-of-Wednesday contact" as its own idea below — same mechanism, one proposal.
- **A standing Boot Camp welcome/serving team** (Kirk's pitch) — guys assigned to garage/elevator/door/name-tags at whichever Sunday service they personally attend, ~15 min commitment. Well received. *Possible overlap: John's notes independently call for a clear new-guy welcoming system and name "our hospitality team" as critical — same problem Kirk's solving, but John's framing is the general church hospitality team rather than a Boot-Camp-specific crew. Worth checking whether these are one proposal or two before finalizing.*
- **Broader "life outside Boot Camp" opportunities** — the post-session mingle idea widens per John's notes: not just a 15-30 min after-session window, but fun activities, service projects, and other informal gatherings generally. Guys want both fun and mission, not just cross-table small talk.
- **Non-church mentorship add-on** (Nico's idea) — practical life-skill help (taxes, cars, career) alongside the spiritual side. Not mentioned in John's notes either way.
- **A side-by-side "build something together" activity** for the mingle time (Brandon Geddes) — rather than face-to-face conversation, something the group does with their hands. Not mentioned in John's notes either way.
- **Food cost sustainability** — new from John's notes (7/22), not raised live at all: everyone agrees a shared meal builds community, but the church shouldn't have to eat the full cost every week. Need to brainstorm how guys themselves can contribute.

## What Connor's on the hook for

- Designing a system so first-time guys get routed to a table that's actually open to a new person, instead of walking in cold to an already-set table.
- Pulling a new-to-Grace / new-believer headcount from last session's table leaders.

## John Slye's notes (email, 7/22) — how they line up

John sent his own written summary of the lunch on 7/22, independent of this transcript-based recap. Cross-checked against the room's discussion above:

- **Corroborates:** relationships-over-content as the top priority (matches Brian Sulc's opening point and Mike Riggs's closing charge), keeping stage stories over longer teaching, the leader/co-leader idea, and the need for a deliberate new-guy welcoming strategy.
- **Adds:** icebreakers as their own focus area, prep guidelines for story-tellers, broadening "outside Boot Camp" to include service/mission (not just a post-session mingle), naming the church's general hospitality team as the lever for new-guy placement, and food-cost sustainability — a topic that never came up live.
- **Contradicted, now resolved:** one primary question per table vs. the room's 1-3 — Connor decided to default to John's notes (one question). See "What we're changing" above.

*Not carried into the leader-facing summary: John's note that Grace is close to hiring a dedicated events person to run the Boot Camp logistics system Connor builds — internal staffing context, not something the table leaders need yet.*

## Best line of the day

Mike Riggs's closing charge: *"Don't let Wednesday night be the only time you communicate with somebody who's at your table... Jesus didn't just go to church with guys. He walked through fields with them."*
