**Take Cluster A, synthesised — not the verbatim row.**

Why this one:

- **Top by votes (21) and top by cluster weight (37).** It wins on both readings, so no one has to be talked out of the number one.
- **It is the Week 2 question.** John 14:16–17 and 14:26 are the Advocate teaching and reminding; the week's practice word is **DISCOVER**. John is already preparing this text — the QOTN answer and the message reinforce instead of competing.
- **The synthesis is honest, not a dodge.** Three separately submitted questions (21 + 2 + 1) are asking the same thing in different words, and the newest one — submitted **at 1:18 PM today** — is the sharpest phrasing of it. Merging them is Connor's stated rule working exactly as designed, and it lets a question from *after* the broadcast into the answer despite the age bias.

**The legitimate alternative, and it is not a wrong answer:** Cluster B's *"How do I not worry when everything is actually falling apart right now?"* (18 votes) also anchors inside Week 2 — John 14:27 — and is the most pastorally urgent thing on the board. **Suggested resolution: give it to John as the message, not the QOTN.** 14:27 is in his passage regardless; he can answer it from the pulpit and spend the Q&A on Cluster A. That covers both of the night's two heaviest clusters without a second Q&A slot.

**Hold Cluster C for a dedicated night.** *"Is Jesus really the only way?"* at 14 votes is the strongest single non-A question and the clearest proof the mechanic works — but Week 2's text does not serve it, and 10–12 prepared minutes with no follow-ups is the wrong container for it. It is the natural anchor for a week where John can build the message around it. **It should not be allowed to quietly age out** — see the pruning note below.
