> [!important] Anchor concept — "Content and Connection" *(John, 2026-06-16)*
> The service is held together by one idea: it can't be just content and it can't be just connection — it must be both. John's image: a punching-bag clown weighted at the base — you can experiment freely ("keep punching") because content + connection always pulls it back upright. Everything below is an experiment anchored to that.

**Format philosophy (vs. Sunday morning): slower, quieter, more intimate** *(2026-06-16)*

- **Worship:** 1–2 people on stage (not the 6–8 of the morning); longer, quieter — roughly **20–22 min** of music (morning is ~15).
- **Host / MC:** a dedicated, more prominent host than the morning — provides continuity and may invite mid-song connection ("turn and meet 5 people").
- **Scripture reading — added to the host role** *(John, 2026-08-17; Connor accepted)*: the host reads the **entire Bible passage for the message** before the speaker begins. John's example: 9/13's text would be **John 14:1-14**.
- **Message:** given at a slower pace; more open-ended/curiosity prompts woven in for use after the service.
- **Close:** the speaker stays and sits with the host for a Q&A drawn from text-in questions (the "bump a question to the top" mechanic), then transitions into discussion/connection. The "landing" will differ week to week.

**Experimental add-ons (test week-to-week, anchored to content + connection):**

- Text-in Q&A (large group) with top questions surfaced/teased for the following week
- Facilitated small-group discussion (open-ended, low/no Bible-study prep; first-come, up to ~12/table)
- Round-table dinner with the speaker
- Shared meal (e.g., a recurring "Sunday pizza" — Timber Pizza available Sunday nights) — *deferred: no food for the first few trials (Connor, 2026-06-17); add later*
- Sports (Sunday-night game on TV) or a dance partnership (country line dancing group that contacted John)
- **Name tags every week** — John endorsed this as a connection/level-setter so newcomers don't feel like "the new kid"; pair with stated reasoning ("we all have a deep need for connection")

> [!note] Sequencing idea (John)
> Build a multi-week game plan ("week 1 we try X, week 2 we try Y"), tell attendees you're experimenting, collect data each week (Have you been before? What did you like? What do you want next week?). Don't try to nail it all on Sept 20 — but define the minimum that makes it a real service.

## 🎙️ Q&A + Livestream — locked 2026-08-12

Source: [[Transcripts/2026-08-12 16.49 Connor–John Connect-Plan Call — Q&A Locked & Tables Pulled]] (Connor + John Slye, the 4:00 PM Connect-plan Zoom).

**The Q&A runs on prepared answers, not live ones.** One question of the night, voted to the top the previous Sunday, teased ahead as the reason to come; **the staff works out the articulation of the answer during the week.** No spontaneously answered questions in the first eight weeks. This is not timidity — John reported a staff member saying flatly *"Hey, no, I'm not doing that"* about answering cold from a stage, and preparation is what keeps the responder roster wider than one person.

**Live submission still happens, and it is the point.** The queue goes up on the screens during the transition out of the message — the window that will draw the most submissions. John: *"that's where the juice is. That's where you get the curiosity up."* The **host pair vamps off the questions climbing the board**, reading them out and reacting, and then nuances the top question before handing it to the responder, since the asker is not on stage. The reasoning is Derek's own story, relayed by John: the church that reached him in his 20s never answered his questions but **read them out loud**, and the acknowledgment was what worked.

**Screening lives in the booth, never in the host chair** — the host pair cannot vamp and screen at once. ✅ **Assigned 2026-08-22: Michael Vacca**, the Sunday-night ProPresenter operator, accepted the seat on the 8/22 call — screening stacks onto the ProPresenter seat because the Q&A runs after the message. His one condition: **he does not pick the question.** He filters submissions (a checkmark or an X as each arrives) and edits out what should not reach the screen; the host pair still teases the top question out of the board. Jean Paul Tauler was ruled out 8/17 on capacity (he is on audio on a live night). Screening posture agreed on the same call: **not "1984 about it"** at first — edge questions can pass, since they surface only on phones for up-voting and it is the vote that decides what reaches the big screens. Stated limit: *"we have the ability to monitor that very closely. I do not think that we have the ability to completely filter it out."* Realistic exposure is slurs and deliberate offense, not sex questions. **Screen count refined 2026-08-22: the top 5–6 up-voted questions reach the big screens** (the 8/12 call said ~10); everything else lives on phones. > [!success] Follow-up questions — settled 2026-08-17

> **The hole Connor named:** if the host pair's banter is spent mining questions for *next* week and it happens *before* the question of the night is answered, then the questions the answer itself provokes — *"did all the animals go two by two? were there dinosaurs?"* — have nowhere to go. *"Those questions might fall through the cracks."*
>
> **Live follow-ups are out, and so is the stage-side circle.** John floated dismissing the formal portion and sitting in a small circle at the stage with whoever wanted to keep talking. Connor: *"I'm reluctant to even mention that there's an opportunity for live follow-up questions even in a smaller format."* His three reasons: Boot Camp did it successfully once and never again, it can *"get hairy or just be a drag on the speaker,"* and the speakers have homes to get to — *"Becky and Krista want their men back home."* John accepted it.
>
> **What changes instead is the banter's job.** Not *"let's use this time to find the question for next week"* but *"let's observe what some of the questions may have been this week to spark the questions next week — and please continue to submit your questions throughout the night."* Submission stays open all night rather than closing at the transition.
>
> **The voted question is never announced from the stage on the way out.** *"We don't need to say as people are leaving, by the way, here's the question next week."* It resolves at midnight and goes out midweek on John's broadcast list instead — see § Marketing & Comms.
>
> **QOTN answer length reconfirmed: 10-12 minutes.**
>
> **The dependency this creates is accepted, not overlooked** *(Connor, 2026-08-17)*: with no live follow-ups, every unanswered question now depends on connect time working. *"Live follow up is too much of a risk. If connection time underperforms, we will have to rework it until it performs well. The goal is connection time, if it fails, we fail."*
>
> **John's addition, and it is the substantive one: the speaker is not supposed to contain all the answers.** *"What if we encourage people to talk amongst themselves… rather than the talking head up front somehow containing all the answers… we can all do this together."* Connor called it better than his own framing — his prior thinking had connect-time discussion responding to the **message**; anchoring it to the **QOTN** is *"just as fair, if not better."* Mechanic: **leave the top questions up on the screen** during connect time. *"We don't need to tell people what to talk about, but we can certainly give them ideas"* — drawn from the message, the QOTN, and potential future QOTNs.
