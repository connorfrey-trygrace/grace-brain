## 🎛️ The five wiggle-room buckets

### 1 · Worship  *(variable: length + texture)*
Length is the main lever here.

- **Length:** short set (~15 min) · standard (~20–22) · extended/reflective (~25–30)
- **Texture:** single guitar + vocal · piano-led · quiet full band · acoustic duo · worship + congregational reading/liturgy
- **Placement:** front-loaded only · or a **response set after the message** (overlaps Message follow-up)
- **Embedded moment:** host pauses mid-song for a connection cue (overlaps Community)

### 2 · Message follow-up / commentary  *(variable: what happens right after the message)*
Tags: 🟢 ≈3–10 min · 🟡 ≈12–20 · 🔴 ≈25–45.

- Dismiss to lobby / no formal follow-up 🟢
- "One takeaway to your neighbor" 🟢
- Host + speaker **live Q&A** on stools (top text-in questions) 🟡 *(John's favorite texture)*
- Single-prompt small-group discussion 🟡
- **Panel** — 2–3 leaders field questions 🔴
- Testimony / open-mic tied to the message 🟡
- **Facilitated small-group discussion**, multi-prompt 🔴
- Workshop / activity on the theme 🔴
- Response worship set in place of talk 🟡 *(overlaps Worship)*

### 3 · Prayer  *(variable, can flex inside the follow-up)*
Tags show added runtime; "parallel" runs during dismissal (~0 added).

- Off / informal · pairs or triads in seats (+3–5) · **integrated groups of 3–4, share + pray** (+8–12, *permission to observe / be prayed for*) · corporate guided from stage (+3–5) · designated prayer team at side (parallel) · prayer stations (parallel) · woven into communion (folded) · text-in prayer requests (parallel)

### 4 · Community  *(variable — note: part block, part woven layer)*
- **Woven:** name tags · "turn, introduce, learn one thing" · mid-song "meet 5 people" · icebreaker prompt on screen · conversation prompt printed on name tag
- **Block / structural:** table hosts · assigned-ish seating · **random→consistent group progression** (Derek's funnel to lasting community — a multi-week dial, not a one-night choice)

### 5 · Layout  *(variable: the physical container + the cap)*
- Curtains pulled (~3 min; can feel "walled") · pull back 150 chairs (more space, more labor) · rectangle tables in back / swivel rows · all rounds (caps ~180 — avoid as default) · lounge / coffee-shop front third + rows · theater rows (max scale)

## ⏱️ Timeline templates (driven by Worship length + Follow-up size + Prayer)

Message is fixed (~27 min), so total length is set by three buckets. Service starts 6:00.

| Template | Worship | Follow-up | Prayer | Ends ~ | Use when |
|---|--|--|--|--|--|
| **TIGHT** | ~15 | 🟢 ≤10 | pairs (+4) | ~7:05 | Holiday/low-turnout, thin labor, reset weeks |
| **STANDARD** | ~20–22 | 🟡 12–20 | guided (+5) | ~7:20 | Default (matches [[Events/2026-09-20-Sunday-Night-Service/2026-09-20-Sunday-Night-Service-Planner|planner]]) |
| **EXTENDED** | ~22 | 🔴 25–40 | integrated (+10) | ~7:45 | Deep-connection nights once people stick around |
| **SOCIAL / EVENT** | ~15 | dismiss | stations (parallel) | service ~7:05, social to ~8:00 | Game / dance / meal — opt-in stay |

> [!warning] Timing trap (your point)
> You can't swap a 🔴 follow-up for a 🟢 and keep the same night — you'd leave a 20-minute hole. A real swap means either **changing templates and ending earlier on purpose** (say so from stage) or **rebalancing** (lengthen worship or push it into a social block). Worship's variable length is your main shock absorber here.

### What the bucket owns

- **The in-room next step.** One named thing a person can do on a Sunday night that continues past 8:00 PM. Not built yet → [[Archive/Actions/Connor - Name the in-room next step for Sunday night weeks 1-8 (off-ramp bucket)]].
- **The capture mechanic.** Tap-to-connect if Heather lands it; a line on the feedback form if she doesn't. The fallback matters more than the tech — a QR already on every chair back can carry "want in on something ongoing?" for free.
- **The handoff.** Alexis Hendrickson from December, and whoever surfaces as a leader before then.

### Its metric — one number, and it is deliberately not stay-rate

**Off-ramp rate = people who took a named next step ÷ attendance.** Weekly, alongside the other four.

Stay-rate cannot stand in for this. Stay-rate is high when the room is warm and the night is fun; it says nothing about whether anyone is still connected on a Tuesday. **A night can score 60% stay-rate and 0% off-ramp, and that is a specific, fixable failure with a different remedy than a bad connect format.** Reported as 0 until the mechanic exists — a blank is data, and it keeps the gap visible on every weekly read instead of surfacing in December.

One clarification baked in above: **Community is half woven-layer, half block.** Worth keeping those separate in your head — name tags (woven) and table-group strategy (block) are different kinds of decisions.

What I did *not* add as buckets (and why): Message format/length (you've fixed it — good), and "timing" itself (it's an output of the buckets, captured by templates).

## 📱 Text-in / upvote question software — comparison (researched 7/6)

Constraints Connor set: needs **live on-screen display** (projected during service, not just host-side), **paid tier OK** if clearly better, and **capacity ≥ 500** (raised 7/6 from the ~250 police-officer threshold noted elsewhere in this doc). No native Rock feature for this — Rock doesn't do live text-in Q&A/upvoting.

| Tool | Free tier | Paid tier w/ Q&A + upvote | Cost to cover 500+ | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AhaSlides** | 50 participants, Q&A included | **Pro: ~$16/mo** ($15.95/mo; annual is **$191.40 = 12 × $15.95 exactly — no annual discount**, confirmed by the 2026-08-18 receipt) — advertised as **unlimited** participants | **~$16/mo** | Clear winner at 500+. "Unlimited" is a marketing claim worth confirming with AhaSlides support before committing (fair-use caps sometimes hide behind that word) — but even their named "Conferences" use case cites up to 2,500, well past your need. Nonprofit/edu rate may cut this further (unconfirmed %). |
| **Slido** | 100 participants | Engage (200 cap, ~$12.50/mo) → Professional (300 cap) → **Enterprise (5,000 cap, ~$1,800/yr = $150/mo)** | **~$150/mo** | Professional's 300-participant ceiling is still short of 500 — you'd be pushed straight to Enterprise. ~10x AhaSlides' cost for the capacity you need. |
| **Mentimeter** | Free, but **no Q&A upvoting** at all | Pro: $28/mo — Q&A + upvote only unlocks here | ~$28/mo+ (participant cap at Pro unconfirmed) | Upvoting gated behind Pro regardless of size; worse cost-to-feature ratio than AhaSlides even before capacity is a factor. |
