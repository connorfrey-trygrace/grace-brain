# Cohost Cheat Sheet: Why Does God Allow Suffering?

All questions come straight from the AhaSlides board, ranked by votes. Each follow-up is another board question on the same thread.

---

**1. If God is always with us and loves us, why do many people go through severe tragedies and traumas to include being victims of violent crimes?** *(18 votes)*
- → My greatest question always goes back to pain. If there is a loving God, why do such awful things happen to innocent people. Things like child abuse, mass catastrophes, bullying? *(Christine)*
- → Why do bad things happen to good people?
- → If God is all-powerful and perfectly good, why do innocent animals experience pain and suffering?

**2. What do you do if you are losing faith that God cares about you?** *(3 votes)*
- → What do you do when you believe God does not care about you anymore?

**3. God never gives us more than we can handle. What's our prayer when He does?** *(2 votes)*
- → Why does God let our loved ones suffer or die tragically at times?

**4. I think back on my life and if tragedy befell me before I came into the church, would I have been doomed? And what about those that never had that opportunity.** *(2 votes, James)*
