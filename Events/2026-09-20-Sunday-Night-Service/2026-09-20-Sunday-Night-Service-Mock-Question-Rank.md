## The two that got pulled in screening

**This is the part worth demoing.** Both were submitted; neither reaches the screen; one of them would have ranked #2.

| Votes it had | Question | Why it was pulled |
|---:|---|---|
| **26** | Why does Grace spend money on a fourth service when the 11:30 is half empty? | On-topic *shape*, congregational-politics payload, and it names a decision rather than the text. It is a **fair question in the wrong room** — a Q&A after a message is not where a budget decision gets litigated. Pull, and answer it properly somewhere it can actually be answered. |
| 8 | I've been praying for my mom's cancer for two years and there's no peace and no advocate. Where is he? | **Pull, and act on it.** Not because it is a bad question — because it is a private crisis disclosed in public, and the asker may not realize 75 people just heard it. Get a name if the platform gives one, and get a pastor to them this week. Never read this from a stage. |

> ⭐ **The #2-with-26-votes case is the whole reason screening exists.** The vote count is the room's enthusiasm, not the room's judgment, and the mechanic has to be able to overrule it. If the volunteers take one thing from the demo, it is that **votes rank; a human decides.**

## What the demo should show at the lunch — four beats, ~3 minutes

1. **Submit.** Two or three people in the room put a question in from their phones off the seat-back/screen QR. Shows the friction (or lack of it) honestly.
2. **Watch it rank.** Votes move the order live. This is the part that sells the mechanic — nobody has to be told why it's better than index cards.
3. **Show the cut line.** Six questions show; the rest don't. Name the number out loud so it isn't a mystery on launch night.
4. **Pull the budget question.** Do it live, in front of them, and say why. That single move establishes the norm the whole mechanic depends on and pre-empts the "you're censoring questions" read before anyone forms it.

## Open decisions the demo should surface, not paper over

- **How many show on screen?** Six is what this mock assumes. Six is the number to defend or change — not to leave undecided until the room is full.
- **Who is the screener on launch night?** Michael Vacca accepted the screener seat 2026-08-22. **Backup is unnamed.**
- **Who is the tiebreaker?** Someone has to make the call on a #2-with-26-votes in real time. Name that person on **8/30**, not on 9/20 — the same open item the Noah's Ark queue raises.
- **Can the screener watch and pull at once?** If the queue and the projected view are the same window, they cannot. **Verify in the software at the Thu 9/3 auditorium screen test**, which is the only in-room test before 9/13.

## Setup notes for building it in AhaSlides

- Load the 11 screen questions plus the 2 pulled ones as a test event, with the vote counts above pre-seeded, so the rank is already interesting when the deck reaches this slide. A cold board with zeros demos nothing.
- Keep the pulled pair **in the queue, unpublished** — the demo needs them visible to Connor and invisible to the screen.
- Screen view: top 6 only, vote counts shown. Volunteers should see that the count is public; it changes how they think about the cut line.
