Recap from Sunday's formation lunch. Sorry you couldn't be there — here's all of it.

**Service structure**
- Worship, host reads the message passage, message, connection opportunity, off-ramp
- Same message as Sunday morning
- Slower and quieter than the morning, smaller band

**Timing**
- 5:00 — volunteers arrive, new folks get brought up to speed
- 5:30 — soundcheck done, doors open
- 6:00 — service
- ~7:30 — off-ramp

**Hosts**
- New role, nothing like it in the morning
- Welcome, read the full passage before the message, then after the message read the questions coming in and set up the question of the night
- Two hosts a week. Pairs don't have to be permanent
- Practice the passage out loud. It's harder than it looks

**Question of the night**
- Submitted by QR code, everyone upvotes, top ones go on the screens
- Anonymous by default, name optional
- Screening is slurs and swears, nothing else. Michael has the booth. I'll screen until launch, including midweek
- One question a week, answered in 10–12 minutes by the speaker, prepared ahead. No live answers
- The previous Sunday sets the question. John teases it midweek on his text list
- Submission opens 9/13 and never closes
- Running on Aha Slides, not the Grace app

**Connection opportunity — this is the experiment**
- Two variables: where the peer discussion happens (auditorium or breakout rooms), and whether the question of the night lands before or after it
- First two weeks in the auditorium, so we can see how many people actually stay before we size rooms
- Breakout rooms start Oct 4. Brandon and Simba leading
- Every combination runs two Sundays, so each question gets four Sundays of data

**What we're measuring**
- Attendance — ushers count, same as the morning
- Stay rate — how many are still in the room at the question of the night
- Feedback form — same questions every week, so we can compare

**Feedback form, two additions**
- "Is there a question you're dying to have answered?"
- "Are you interested in leading or serving?" — I'll email everyone who says yes

**Worship**
- Seth leading. Opens with a medley of songs people know, a few seconds on why we sing at all, then 2–3 songs tied to that week's message
- Deliberate stillness built in — 60 seconds to two minutes to just breathe
- About 20 minutes
- Rhett Walker Oct 4, solo acoustic for the evening. Kim Walker-Smith pending for Oct 11

**Off-ramp**
- Nobody has to go home at 7:20
- Inspira, weekly, Sunday night country swing, $10. Skylar has it lined up
- Want more than one — game night, dinner, whatever people will actually lead
- Looking for off-ramp leaders

**Still open**
- Volunteer captains
- Greeter lead
- Usher lead
- Connection / room leads
- Host pairs
- Off-ramp activity leaders

Any of those grab you? And if you know someone at Grace who'd be great at one, send them my way.

Thanks for being part of this.
