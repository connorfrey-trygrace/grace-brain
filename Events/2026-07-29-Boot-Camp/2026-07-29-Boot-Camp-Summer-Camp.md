## 🎯 Program (deliberately light)

The whole point is community, not a class. Keep structure to a single anchor:

- **Food first** — let guys eat and mingle; the cookout is the connection vehicle.
- **One shared question**, one round, at whatever tables/circles form naturally — e.g. *"What's slipped since we finished boot camp?"* or *"Where's the discipline holding this summer, and where's it not?"* This is the "use it or lose it" check-in in disguise and gives men a doorway into depth without reconvening the curriculum.
- **Short vision cast for the fall relaunch** (optional, 2–3 min) — remind people boot camp exists and that it's coming back in September; surface who might join the fall vision-setting team.

No multi-table rotation, no workbook. New guys can sit in on the question fine — it's accessible without being built around them.
